## 1. Brand Mission & Core Identity

**CrazzyDocs** transforms complex scientific phenomena, everyday paradoxes, and data-driven discoveries into compelling, high-retention 50–60 second YouTube Shorts.

The brand voice is:
- **Inquisitive**: Always asking "Why?" and uncovering the counter-intuitive mechanism beneath ordinary life.
- **Accurate**: Backed by authoritative data and peer-reviewed research (NASA, IEA, arXiv, Nature).
- **Relatable**: Explaining high-concept ideas through friendly analogies, visual humor, and dynamic motion graphics.
- **Punchy**: Zero wasted words, immediate hooks, continuous visual momentum.

## 4. Logo Display & Clear Space Standards

1. **Aspect Ratio Preservation**: The CrazzyDocs circular logo must always maintain a strict $1:1$ aspect ratio. Stretching or non-uniform scaling is strictly prohibited.
2. **Clear Space**: A minimum clear space of $24\text{ px}$ (at $380\text{ px}$ logo scale) must surround the logo border.
3. **Contrast Requirement**: When placed over video backgrounds, the logo must utilize the circular gold/teal accent ring (`width=6px`) to ensure distinct separation from both light and dark scenes.
4. **Outro Sizing**: In vertical 1080 × 1920 videos, the logo renders at $380\text{ px}$ diameter (centered at `x = 540`, `y = 760`), ensuring prominent visibility on mobile screens without crowding the title.

## 5. In-Video Branding Principles

Branding should feel like an organic stamp of quality, not intrusive advertising:

### Permitted In-Video Branding:
- **Natural Script Integration**: Scripts may occasionally include natural, conversational references in the hook or closing stage:
  - *"Here's something interesting from CrazzyDocs..."*
  - *"Subscribe to CrazzyDocs for your daily dose of unexpected science."*
- **Subtle Visual Watermark (Optional)**: A $120 \times 40\text{ px}$ semi-transparent brand pill placed in the safe upper-right corner (`x: 880`, `y: 120`).
- **Unified Branded Outro**: The official 2.5-second outro appended at the end of every video.

### Prohibited Branding Practices:
- ❌ Do **NOT** force brand shoutouts into every script. Stories must stand on their own merit.
- ❌ Do **NOT** interrupt the narrative flow mid-video with "Don't forget to like and subscribe".
- ❌ Do **NOT** obscure subtitles or mobile safe zones with oversized channel watermarks.
