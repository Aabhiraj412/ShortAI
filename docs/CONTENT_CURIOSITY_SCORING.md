# Content Curiosity Scoring & Topic Discovery Engine
**CrazzyDocs Production Standard — Phase 5, Stage 3.4**

---

## 1. Executive Overview

The CrazzyDocs Content Intelligence Engine transitions video selection from a purely informative historical catalogue to high-velocity, curiosity-driven storytelling designed specifically for YouTube Shorts retention.

---

## 2. Five Diverse Topic Categories

To avoid repetitive content and maintain high audience variety, candidate stories are harvested and categorized across five distinct content pillars:

| Category | Description | Primary Psychological Hook |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category A: Strange But True History** | Bizarre historical occurrences, strange laws, unintended historical collisions. | "How did nobody realize this at the time?" |
| **Category B: Unbelievable Real-Life Stories** | Extreme human survival, bizarre luck, double events, incredible coincidences. | "Is this even physically possible?" |
| **Category C: Science & Technology** | Astonishing discoveries, military secrets, lost inventions, bizarre experiments. | "Why didn't they teach us this in school?" |
| **Category D: Corporate & Financial Blunders** | Extreme corporate negligence, disastrous mergers, absurd financial heists. | "How could smart executives do something so foolish?" |
| **Category E: Mysteries & Unexplained Events** | Historical enigmas, declassified anomalies, baffling disappearances. | "What actually happened here?" |

---

## 3. Six-Dimensional Curiosity Scoring Formula

Every story candidate is systematically evaluated across six weighted dimensions on a 1.0 to 10.0 scale:

$$\text{Total Score} = 0.25 \cdot C + 0.20 \cdot S + 0.20 \cdot N + 0.15 \cdot V + 0.10 \cdot E + 0.10 \cdot F$$

Where:
- **$C$ (Curiosity Potential — 25%):** How strongly does the core premise compel the viewer to stop scrolling? Does it create an immediate question that demands an answer?
- **$S$ (Surprise Factor — 20%):** Does the story violate common-sense expectations or deliver a counter-intuitive twist?
- **$N$ (Narrative Arc / Storytelling — 20%):** Can the story be structured into a 6-stage arc (Hook $\to$ Context $\to$ Escalation $\to$ Twist $\to$ Payoff $\to$ Resolution) within 50–55 seconds?
- **$V$ (Visual Potential — 15%):** Are there authentic, compelling, high-contrast historical photographs, maps, schematics, and archival documents available in the public domain?
- **$E$ (Emotional Resonance — 10%):** Does the viewer feel awe, tension, shock, admiration, or relief?
- **$F$ (Factual Reliability — 10%):** Is the story backed by undisputed primary sources, government archives, or verified historical records?

### Mandatory Factual Gate
Any candidate scoring below **8.0 / 10.0** on Factual Reliability ($F$) is **instantly disqualified**, regardless of curiosity score. CrazzyDocs never publishes urban legends, exaggerated myths, or unverified claims.

## 5. Five Hook Directions & Selection Protocol

For every top-ranked candidate, five distinct hook directions are formulated and ranked:

1. **Shocking Revelation:** Stating an astounding verified fact that feels impossible.
2. **Paradox / Contradiction:** Highlighting two conflicting truths occurring simultaneously.
3. **Unexpected Consequence:** Beginning with a mundane decision that triggered catastrophe or wonder.
4. **Intriguing Question:** Forcing the viewer's brain to attempt solving a riddle.
5. **Extraordinary Human Decision:** Focusing on an impossible choice made under extreme pressure.

### Tested Hook Variations for Tsutomu Yamaguchi
- *Direction 1 (Shocking Revelation):* **"He survived the atomic blast in Hiroshima. Three days later, he was in Nagasaki."** (Score: 9.84 / 10 — WINNER)
- *Direction 2 (Paradox / Contradiction):* "He caught the last train escaping nuclear destruction, straight into ground zero of the second bomb." (Score: 9.40 / 10)
- *Direction 3 (Unexpected Consequence):* "His boss laughed and said one bomb couldn't destroy a city—just as the window exploded in blinding white fire." (Score: 9.65 / 10)
- *Direction 4 (Intriguing Question):* "What are the astronomical odds of surviving history's only two wartime nuclear strikes?" (Score: 8.90 / 10)
- *Direction 5 (Extraordinary Decision):* "Burned and deafened by the first atomic bomb, he dragged himself to work three days later to report for duty." (Score: 9.25 / 10)
