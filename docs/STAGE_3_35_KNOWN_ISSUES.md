# Stage 3.35: known issues and limits

| # | Item | Impact | Status / next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Old OAuth authorization codes in `logs/dashboard_service.log`** (2026-11-12 to 2026-11-21) | None in practice: the codes are single-use and already exchanged | The OAuth library logger is now at WARNING, so no new codes are logged. The log itself was left untouched (user-owned); delete or rotate it at your discretion. |
| 2 | **Learning sample too small for claims** | Findings are labelled *preliminary*; no recommendation is a claim | 1 video has first-7-days metrics, and 30 are needed. Expected around 2026-11-28 as videos reach day 8. |
| 3 | **Very low audience volume** (134 lifetime views; the most-viewed video has 12) | Even at 30 videos, effects will have wide confidence intervals | The thresholds (at least 10 per group, a 15 % relative effect, permutation p-values) are deliberately strict; nothing is applied automatically. |
| 4 | **Retention curves unavailable** | Retention summaries stay `not_yet_populated` | YouTube returns no audience-retention curve at this view count. The collector asks again on each first-7-days collection. |
| 5 | **Impressions and impression CTR** | Cannot be learned from | Not offered by the YouTube Analytics API (YouTube Studio only). Not collected and not estimated. |
| 6 | **Footage availability depends on the subject** | Abstract or very specific subjects (a named molecule, a statistic) get few relevant stock clips | By design: relevance and licence come first, and photo or vector scenes remain the fallback. The 40–60 % footage share is a flexible target, not a quota. Data, comparison and process scenes never take footage. |
| 7 | **Pixabay stock footage is matched on tags** | Tag lists are short; a clip can match the subject words but show a generic scene | It must share anchor words with the subject **and** the scene, and pass duration, resolution, decode and black-frame checks. The manifest records the matched terms for every clip, so mismatches can be audited. |
| 8 | **Pexels is not configured** | One fewer stock library | Optional. Add `PEXELS_API_KEY` to `.env.local` to enable it. Nothing depends on it. |
| 9 | **Internet Archive footage not integrated** | — | Licence metadata varies per item, so automated licence verification is unreliable. Left as a candidate. |
| 10 | **Storyboard assets and animations are produced but unused by Pop Info renders** (about 100 s of work per cycle) | Wasted time per cycle; no visual effect | Not changed in 3.35, because removing a pipeline stage touches the orchestrator's checkpoints. Candidate for a later reliability stage. |
| 11 | **Edge TTS remains the single neural voice** | A long network outage still fails a cycle's voiceover (1 final failure in 691 syntheses, on 2026-11-17) | Longer backoff (2, 5, 10 s) now. A Windows SAPI fallback exists but is **off** (`CRAZZYDOCS_TTS_FALLBACK=sapi`), because its robotic voice is below the channel's standard. Enabling it is an operator decision. |
| 12 | **Analytics scope self-heal happens at the first post-deployment collection** | Until then the stored scope list still lacks the analytics scope name | The live collection run after deployment verifies and records it (scope name only). |
| 13 | **Known test failures** (unchanged) | — | `test_dashboard_html_page_served`, `test_end_to_end_orchestration_run`, both `test_youtube_branding`. |
| 15 | **The 3.28 photo selector can accept a differently qualified subject** (a "Wind turbine blades" photo was chosen for a gas-turbine video) | Occasional loosely related photographs | Pre-existing. The new qualifier-conflict rule protects **footage** only; applying it to photo selection is a small follow-up, left out of 3.35 to avoid changing the photo pipeline unmeasured. |
| 14 | **Footage adds render time and download traffic** | A few seconds of downloads per clip (cached, content-addressed, 80 MB cap per file) and frame extraction | Measured in the controlled preview (`STAGE_3_35_VISUAL_ASSET_AUDIT.md` §5). `CRAZZYDOCS_FOOTAGE=off` disables footage without a code change. |
