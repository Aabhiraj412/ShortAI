- **Central Dominant Image:** $y \in [310, 1490]$ ($1180\text{px}$ height = $61.5\%$ of total canvas height, ensuring imagery dominates).
- **Bottom Safe Clearance:** $y \le 1660\text{px}$. Lower editorial power-phrase pills are rendered at $y \in [1525, 1635]$.
- **Horizontal Margin:** $x \in [60, 1020]$ ($60\text{px}$ clearance on both left and right edges).
